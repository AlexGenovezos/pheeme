    Your keyboard runs this demo.

    Fast is the feature.

    Your mail lives in a local cache, so opening a thread, searching, and archiving don't wait on a server. The goal is that nothing you do takes longer than a keystroke. When something's slow, that's a bug.

    Hands stay on the keys.

    Every action has a shortcut, and the shortcuts you already know from Vim and Gmail work on day one.

    j kMove through messages
    eMark done and move on
    r aReply, reply all
    /Search everything
    ⌘ KRun any command

    Yours to read and fork.

    Your inbox is the most private thing on your computer. You should be able to read every line of the app that opens it. pheeme is open source, so you can.

    ~ gh repo clone AlexGenovezos/pheeme
~ cd pheeme
~/pheeme
