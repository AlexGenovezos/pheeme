<!-- Release notes for v0.19. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Google Calendar and Google Tasks arrive in Pheeme: every account's calendars next to your mail, invites answered from the email, and your tasks in a side panel.

**Calendar**
- A **Mail | Calendar** switch sits at the top of the sidebar (`mod+1` / `mod+2`). Pheeme remembers which one you had open.
- Day, Week and Month views show every account's calendars together, in Google's colours, with a mini month for jumping to any date and a tick to show or hide each calendar. The keys match Google Calendar: `d`, `w`, `m` for the views, `t` for today, `j` / `k` for next and previous, `g` to go to a date.
- Each account asks once for calendar access the first time you open Calendar. If you use your own Google Cloud project, turn on the Google Calendar API and Google Tasks API there; Pheeme links you to the right page, and the setup guide now covers both.
- Click an event for its details, guests and their answers, **Join with Google Meet**, and Yes / Maybe / No.

**Creating and changing events**
- Click or drag on an empty time (or press `c`, or **Create**) to add an event; **More options** opens the full editor: all day, time zones, repeat (with Custom), guests, Meet, place, description, calendar, colour, reminders, busy/free and visibility.
- Drag an event to move it, drag its bottom edge to change its length, or press `e` to edit and `Delete` to delete it. The event's menu has Duplicate and Copy to another calendar.
- Changing a repeating event asks whether it's for this event, this and following events, or all of them; when there are guests, Pheeme asks whether to email them.
- Press `/` in Calendar to search your events.
- Reminders arrive as desktop notifications, following your notification schedule; **Event reminders** in Settings › Notifications turns them off. Clicking one opens the event.

**Invites in Mail**
- An invitation email shows a card at the top: when and where, who's going, a mini view of your day around it that names any clash, and Yes / Maybe / No. Updated and cancelled invites say so.
- **Open in Calendar** shows that day with the email pinned beside it.
- **Create event** (`shift+E`) turns any email into an event: the subject becomes the title, the people in the thread become guests, and a time mentioned in the email is filled in.

**Tasks**
- The **Tasks** panel at the right edge (`mod+shift+t`) shows every account's task lists, in Mail and Calendar. Add, edit, tick off, delete, drag to reorder, indent (`mod+]` / `mod+[`), and move tasks between lists; create, rename and delete lists.
- Tasks with a date also show on the calendar, where you can tick them off. The calendar sidebar picks which lists show there.

**Mail**
- Right-click an inbox tab to mark everything in it as read.

**Fixes**
- **Restart to update** now installs the newest release, instead of installing an older one and then asking to update again.

**Heads-up**
- This version updates the local mail cache (`012_tasks.sql`, `013_calendar_reminders.sql`). Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
