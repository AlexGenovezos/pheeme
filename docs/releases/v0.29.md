Pheeme plans your day's overdue tasks with you, searches with every Gmail operator, saves event templates, and lets you colour and order your accounts.

**Tasks**
- **Plan today**: the first time you use Pheeme each day, if anything is overdue or due today, it walks those tasks one at a time, oldest first. Each gets a decision with one key: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Pick a day**, **Book a time**, **Done** or **Drop** (with Undo). **Stop for today** (Escape) leaves the rest. Open it again from the palette or the Tasks panel.
- The Tasks panel opens with **Today**: every open task due today or earlier, across accounts.
- **Book a time** sets the due day and opens Google Calendar on the web there, where you drag the task onto an hour. Google's task blocks then show in Pheeme's calendar as their task, with a tick that completes it, and **Mark completed** and **Open in Google Tasks** in the popover.

**Search**
- **Search options** (the sliders button in the search box) opens Gmail's advanced search as a form, with a field for every operator Gmail has: from, to, subject, label, category, `is:`, `has:` (attachment, Drive, every star…), filename, list, dates, size, header and more. It reads what you typed and writes the search back; anything it can't split apart stays in "Has the words" as written.
- Typing suggests operators and their values (`ha` → `has:`, `label:` → your labels); `Tab` completes it.
- A **Has attachment** checkbox in the form, and **Unread only** stays in the toolbar while searching.
- The search box stays one row: starting a search no longer pushes the list down.

**Calendar**
- **Event templates**: an event's **Options › Save as template…** keeps its guests, place, calendar, video call and reminders under a name. A quick add sentence that starts with the name uses it ("gym friday at 7"), the templates show as buttons under quick add, the palette has "New event from template: …", and Settings › Calendar renames and deletes them. Templates stay on this computer.
- The weather in Day and Week now looks like it's seen through a window: beads of rain running down the glass, lightning in a storm, snow falling outside, misted glass in fog, and light slanting in on sunny hours. Each scene plays for a few seconds, then winds down instead of freezing.

**Accounts**
- Click the dot before an account's address (or **Change colour…** in its menu) to pick one of 12 colours. The dot, the account rail and the All Inboxes stripe follow it.
- Drag an account's header to reorder, or use **Move up** / **Move down** in its menu. The sidebar, the rail and Settings follow the order.
- `Ctrl+Tab` / `Ctrl+Shift+Tab` step through All Inboxes and each account, staying on the same tab or favourite where the account has it.

**Mail**
- Paste a screenshot or a copied image into a message to attach it.
- A flight number ("AA 0779") is no longer offered as a verification code.

**Heads-up**
- This version updates the local mail cache (`018_event_templates.sql`, `019_account_order.sql`). Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
