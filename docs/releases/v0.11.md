<!-- Release notes for v0.11. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Big mailboxes stay fast, mail is easier to read, and a signed-out account can sign back in without losing its mail.

**Speed**
- **Unread only** and the sidebar and menu bar counts respond right away on large mailboxes. On a 200,000-conversation mailbox, turning on the filter used to take several seconds.
- Typing the first letter of a search shows cached results in under a tenth of a second.
- Diagnostic logs now record slow mailbox loads and syncs, so slowness can be traced from a log file.

**Reading**
- Reading and compose text is larger by default. Pick **Large** in **Settings › Appearance › Text size** for bigger still.
- Secondary text has more contrast and small buttons are easier to hit. **New message** and selecting several conversations are easier to find.
- Each conversation shows its account above the subject. Sender avatars are gone from the list and message headers.
- Error messages in the light theme are readable again.

**Accounts**
- When Google stops accepting an account's sign-in, **Sign in again** appears in **Settings › Accounts** and the sidebar. It reconnects the same account and keeps its mail.

**Keyboard**
- After clicking into an email, `←` returns to the conversation list so `↑` and `↓` move through it again.
- Unread counts keep up as you move through mail quickly.
