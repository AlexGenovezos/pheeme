<!-- Release notes for v0.16. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Sort mail into the right inbox tab and teach Gmail where a sender belongs, preview attachments without downloading them, and links in emails now open on the Mac and Linux.

**Inbox tabs**
- **Move to…** (`v`) now lists Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates and Forums. Pick one and choose **Just this conversation**, or **This and future mail from** the sender, which saves a Gmail filter so their new mail lands there on every device.
- With future mail, **Also move their other mail in the inbox** (on by default) moves that sender's other inbox conversations too. One Undo takes back the mail and the rule.
- Drag conversations, or the checked ones, onto an inbox tab, a label or Trash in the sidebar. Only places that accept the drop light up, and `Esc` cancels the drag.
- **Settings › Sender rules** lists each account's rules, and you can delete them there.
- Rules need one more Gmail permission. Accounts connected before this version show **Sign in again** in the move dialog. Moving a single conversation works without it.

**Attachments**
- Click an attachment, or press `Space` on it, to preview it over the app: PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets and CSV files, images and text.
- `←`/`→` flip through the message's attachments, and `Space` or `Esc` closes the preview.
- **Open** hands the file to your default app, and **Download** works as before. Opening a conversation fetches its attachments in the background, so previews usually appear at once.

**Reading mail**
- Links in emails open in your browser or mail app on macOS and Linux. Before, clicking them did nothing.
- Click an open message's header to close it. You can still select the sender, address and date as text.
- The speed reader reads the message on screen, not an older one in the thread, and a short reply reads on into the conversation it quotes. Reopening an email picks up where you left off.
- In the dark theme, the text that stands in for an image that didn't load is readable now.

**Updates**
- Pheeme checks for updates again after your Mac wakes, when you bring the window back, and soon after a failed check. If a newer release ships while an update is waiting, you're offered the newer one.

**Look and feel**
- The accent colour is the ink blue from the app icon instead of green, in light and dark.
- The welcome screen is tidier, with one clean column and buttons of the same size.

**Smaller and leaner**
- The app download is about 40% smaller, with no change to start time or memory.
- Old email bodies no longer pile up in the local cache. Mail older than 180 days keeps its body for 30 days after you open it, then downloads again next time you open it.

**Usage count**
- Pheeme now sends an anonymous ping once a day so we can count active installs: a random install ID, the app version, the OS and the chip type, and nothing about you or your mail. Turn it off on the welcome screen or in **Settings › Diagnostics**.
- Update checks and slow email opens now show up in **Send diagnostics** reports, so we can see what went wrong.

**Heads-up**
- This version updates the local mail cache (`008_body_retention.sql`). Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
