<!-- Release notes for v0.23. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

A new sidebar footer, Mail stats in a window of its own, spell check that works, and fixes to mark-as-read and notifications.

**Sidebar footer**
- The bottom of the sidebar is the same in Mail and Calendar: the Mail | Calendar switch across the top, then Settings, Mail stats and Add account, with **Check for updates** on the right.
- **Check for updates** checks right away and shows the result in the update toast. Once an update has downloaded, the button lights up, and clicking it restarts Pheeme on the new version.
- The account's avatar and email no longer sit at the bottom of the sidebar.

**Mail stats**
- Mail stats now opens in a window of its own, from the sidebar footer or the command palette's **Mail stats** command, instead of as a Settings page.
- The window is the same size as Settings and scrolls as one page: daily mail, Where it lands and Top senders, with 7 days, 30 days, 90 days and 1 year buttons in the header.
- Searching for "stats" in Settings still finds it.

**Compose**
- Spell check works: misspelled words get a red underline as you type, and right-clicking one offers suggestions. On Linux it uses your system's hunspell dictionaries for your languages.

**Mail**
- The conversation toolbar's read icons were the wrong way round. An open envelope now means **Mark as read** and a closed one **Mark as unread**, as in Gmail and on the row's hover buttons.
- Your selection now stays after mark read, mark unread, star or label, so you can select unread mail, mark it read, then archive the same rows. Rows are unchecked only when they leave the list (archive, delete, move).
- Clicking a new-mail notification opens that account's **Primary** inbox with the conversation open and marks it read, instead of opening All Mail and leaving it unread.
