<!-- Release notes for v0.26. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Your calendar can show the weather, meeting guests open a person card, mail comes back out of Spam and Trash, and AI assistants can work with your calendar and tasks.

**Calendar**
- See the weather: Day, Week and Month show each day's forecast with its high and low, and opening an event with a place shows the forecast there on its day. It's off until you turn on Settings › Calendar › **Show weather**, and then Pheeme sends only your home location and the places of events you open to Open-Meteo. **Animate weather**, or the pause button on the forecast, holds it still.
- Click a guest or the organizer in an event to see who they are: photo, company and phone from Google Contacts, recent conversations, upcoming meetings and the meetings you've had together in the last 6 months. **New event** invites them to a meeting at the next half hour, and **Copy address** copies their email.
- In Week view, an event that a later one overlaps part-way down now shows its full title and its time, not one cut-off line.

**Mail**
- In Spam, the Archive button (and `e`) becomes **Not spam**; in Trash it becomes **Move to Inbox**. Both put the conversation back in your inbox, as Gmail does.
- The person card for a sender or recipient now also shows their photo, company and phone, and the meetings you've had together.

**AI assistants (MCP)**
- Assistants can read your calendar, find free time and read your Google Tasks. Two new switches in Settings › MCP, **Change calendar** and **Change tasks** (both off by default), let them create, edit, delete and answer events, and add, edit and complete tasks.
- Drafts an assistant writes carry your signature, chosen the same way as in compose.
- Assistants can count mail by sender, domain, label or month in one step, and archive or mark everything a search finds. Filter reviews point out wanted mail, like orders and bills, that a filter deletes or archives.
- Each assistant may make 120 reads and 30 changes a minute, so a runaway assistant can't flood Gmail.

**Setup**
- On a Mac, **Receive from another device** asks for Local Network access before you paste the code, instead of failing the first time. Until you allow it, the screen offers **Open System Settings**.

**Diagnostics**
- Settings › Diagnostics can include the email on screen in a report (off by default), to help track down an email that shows wrong.
- Reports no longer claim an email took 30 seconds to show on macOS when it didn't.

**Heads-up**
- This version updates the local mail cache. Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
