<!-- Release notes for v0.21. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Mark every email from a sender read at once, swipe through the calendar, a calmer Calendar mode, and big mail changes that no longer get stuck on Gmail's limits.

**Mail**
- Right-click a conversation (or use More actions or the command palette) and pick **Find emails from this sender** or **Find emails with this subject**. While that search shows, **Mark all as read** marks every match read, including ones Pheeme never downloaded.

**Calendar**
- Swipe sideways with two fingers on the trackpad to go to the next or previous week (or day or month); the new days slide in. `←` / `→` work too.
- A calmer look: Day/Week/Month is centred over the days, there are fewer lines, events are tinted with their calendar's colour, and the UI uses the system font (SF Pro on the Mac).
- The sidebar's mini month marks busy days with dots, and a new agenda lists what's left today and the next two days, with "Up next".
- A yearly event moved onto February 29 no longer disappears three years in four, and a repeating event keeps its weekday when its time zone changes.

**Gmail limits**
- Settings › Accounts shows how much of each account's Gmail limit Pheeme used in the last hour, and on what: new mail, older mail, your changes, searches or AI assistants. When Gmail refuses before Pheeme could have reached the limit, it says something else is using it: Pheeme on another device, or another app with the same Google Cloud client.
- A big change, like labelling thousands of conversations, goes to Gmail 25 conversations at a time. When Gmail says to slow down, what was already done stays done, and your other clicks go ahead instead of waiting behind it.

**AI assistants**
- Assistants can label every email matching a search in one step, without reading each conversation and using up Gmail's limit.
- Assistants now know that Move to Inbox also takes mail out of Trash.
