<!-- Release notes for v0.25. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Your cached mail is now encrypted on disk, Drive files and the people you write to come into Pheeme, each Inbox tab can keep its own Unread only, and calendar events are easier to read.

**Security**
- The mail Pheeme keeps on your computer is encrypted: the whole database (bodies, subjects, addresses, drafts, contacts, calendar, tasks and the search index) and every cached or staged attachment. The keys live in your system keychain. Search and offline reading work as before. [How it works](https://github.com/AlexGenovezos/pheeme/blob/main/docs/local-encryption.md)
- If Pheeme can't start (a locked keychain, a missing key, Pheeme already open), it says why in a dialog instead of quietly closing. With a missing or damaged key, **Start again** sets the unreadable cache aside and downloads your mail again.

**Mail**
- Click a sender or recipient to see your recent conversations with them and your upcoming meetings together, with buttons to write to them or save them to Google Contacts.
- In compose, search your Google Drive and insert a link to a file. Save any attachment you receive to My Drive. Pheeme asks for Drive access the first time you use either.
- Settings › Appearance › Layout › **Unread only applies to**: choose **Each mailbox** and every mailbox and Inbox tab remembers its own Unread only, so Primary can show everything while Promotions and Updates show only unread.
- When Gmail slows down syncing, the sidebar shows one calm note (and says when another device may be syncing the same account) instead of an error.
- A list, calendar or Tasks panel that fails to load now says so, with **Try again**, rather than looking empty.

**Calendar**
- Events that overlap get wider cards that partly overlap instead of thin columns, and a card comes forward when you point at it or tab to it.
- Long events in narrow columns show their full title, time and place instead of one cut-off line.
- Calendars you delete or unsubscribe from on Google leave Pheeme's sidebar on the next sync, not up to 15 minutes later.

**Appearance**
- Settings › Appearance › **Sidebar color** for Pheeme Light: **Light** (the default again, with the see-through sidebar back on macOS), **Tinted**, **Ink** or **Dark**.

**Setup**
- Adding an account now ends with **New mail alerts**: turn alerts on for that account or choose Not now.

**Keyboard**
- `shift+F10` or the Menu key opens the menu for the selected email or task. Arrow keys, `Home`/`End` and `PageUp`/`PageDown` move around the month view. **Focus Tasks** is in the command palette.

**Heads-up**
- The first launch encrypts your existing cache, which needs free disk space for a second copy of it. Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
- On Linux, a keychain (GNOME Keyring or KWallet) must be running and unlocked when Pheeme starts.
- Back up your system keychain along with your files: without the key, the local cache can't be read. Your mail is still in Gmail.
