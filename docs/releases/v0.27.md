<!-- Release notes for v0.27. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Pheeme now keeps itself up to date, your calendar shows other time zones and guests' availability, and Settings is tidier.

**Updates**
- Pheeme checks for updates every 30 minutes and downloads them in the background. Once it has gone unused for a while (window hidden for 10 minutes, or behind other windows for an hour, with nothing being written), it saves your drafts, installs the update and restarts out of sight. Quitting installs a downloaded update too.
- Settings › About › **Install updates automatically** (on by default) turns this off.
- The "Check for updates" toast stays one slim line and no longer changes size when its answer arrives.

**Calendar**
- Add up to two other time zones in Settings › Calendar. Day and Week give each one its own column of hours, and an event's popover shows its time in each.
- Type a time in the command palette, like `3pm PT in Athens` or `15:00 London to Tokyo`, and Enter copies the answer.
- When your computer's clock is in a different zone from your calendar, a note at the foot of the sidebar says so and can add your computer's zone in one click.
- **Check availability** in the event editor shows you and each guest as busy time for the day. Click a free spot to move the event there, or press **Next available time** to jump to the first slot that works for everyone. The first time, it asks you to allow access.

**Settings**
- Settings is down from 16 pages to 12, each topic on one page: a new **Reading** page, Sync settings on **Accounts**, Inbox tabs and Sender rules on **Inbox**, and Signatures on **Compose**.
- Accounts that sign in through the same Google Cloud app now share a card, which names the app and the Gmail quota they used together. Each account keeps its own quota chart, since Gmail's limit is per account.
- Tab stays inside Settings instead of moving to the app behind it.

**Fixes**
- A queued change Pheeme couldn't read no longer stops an account from syncing for good. A send caught this way reopens its compose window instead of showing "Sending…" forever.
- Removing an account removes all of it, never half.
- A failed **Save to Downloads** says Pheeme couldn't save the file, instead of naming a cache error.
- The invite card says when it couldn't load your calendar, instead of claiming your day is free.
