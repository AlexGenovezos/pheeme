<!-- Release notes for v0.10. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Get new-mail alerts, keep Pheeme running in the tray, and add personal integrations without maintaining a fork.

**Notifications and tray**
- Turn on alerts per account in **Settings › Notifications**. New unread Primary mail shows the sender, subject and a short preview; click an alert to open its conversation in the right account.
- Initial downloads, search results and resyncs stay silent. Your system's notification settings and Focus or Do Not Disturb control banners and sounds.
- The menu bar or system tray shows your unread inbox conversation count across accounts. Open Pheeme, mark an account's cached mail as read, or quit from its menu.
- Closing the window keeps sync and notifications running while the tray is available. Use **Quit Pheeme** to stop the app; on Linux without a tray host, closing exits normally.

**Unread mail**
- Use **Unread only** to filter the current mailbox. Pheeme remembers the choice separately for each account, label and category during your session.
- Conversations you just read stay in the filtered list until you refresh or leave the mailbox. Searching keeps its own scope; clearing search restores your mailbox's filter.

**Personal integrations**
- Install local mods from **Settings › Mods › Install from folder**, review their requested access, and enable them without rebuilding Pheeme. Installed mods and their preferences survive app updates.
- Mods can add sidebar controls, panels, commands, shortcuts and settings, and connect to external services. This first version does not give mods access to your mail.
- Try the included Counter example, or configure the Spotify example with your own Spotify developer client. See the [mod guide](https://github.com/AlexGenovezos/pheeme/blob/main/docs/mods.md) for installation and setup.

**Writing and reading**
- Sending after the undo window no longer waits behind mailbox sync, and the sent confirmation appears before old-draft cleanup finishes.
- **New message** stays available in the toolbar when the sidebar is collapsed.
- Fixed-width emails fit when you narrow the reading pane, with artwork kept in proportion. GitHub notification email keeps its status headings and icons readable.

**Heads-up**
- This version updates the local mail cache (`005_desktop.sql`). Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
- New macOS builds support Apple Silicon. Windows and Linux builds remain available.
