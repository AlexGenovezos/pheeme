<!-- Release notes for v0.7, written after the fact: v0.7 was published before release notes lived in docs/releases. -->

Pheeme opens where you left off, without a flash.

**Launch**
- Pheeme reopens the mailbox you last had open. If its account or label is gone, it falls back to your inbox.
- No more dark flash for light-theme users: the window appears once the first screen has drawn.
- On macOS, the sidebar uses the system's translucent sidebar material.

**Under the hood**
- Less memory over a long session: sync no longer keeps the ids of every deleted message after the background backfill.
- The groundwork for writing mail (drafts, replies, forwards and undo send) is in place. You'll see it in the next release.
