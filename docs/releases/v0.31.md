<!-- Release notes for v0.31. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Pheeme keeps Spam's tracking images hidden, keeps the mail list quicker after a sync, and fixes a second Pheeme appearing in the Dock after future updates.

**Mail**
- Emails in Spam no longer load remote images on their own, just like Gmail. This stops tracking pixels from telling the sender that your address is real, when you opened the email, or roughly where you are. Choose **Show images** above the email to see them for that message.
- On large mailboxes with several accounts, the sidebar is far less likely to slow the mail list down after each sync.
- The regular full sync finishes sooner, because Pheeme now asks Gmail for the Inbox and tab counts all at once.

**Fixes**
- On macOS, Pheeme comes back in its pinned spot in the Dock after an update restarts it, instead of showing a second copy beside it. This works from the update after this one, because the version being replaced is the one that does the restart.
