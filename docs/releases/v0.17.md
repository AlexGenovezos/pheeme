<!-- Release notes for v0.17. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Settings you can make your own, recipient suggestions as you type, sign-in codes you can copy in one click, and new-mail alerts within about 10 seconds.

**Settings**
- **List density** in Settings › Appearance: Compact shows two lines per conversation (sender and subject) so more fit on screen.
- Turn off the **reading pane** to give the list the whole window. `j`/`k` move a highlight, `x` selects, `Enter` opens the conversation full width, and Back or `u` returns to the list.
- **Default reply** in Settings › Compose picks whether Reply or Reply all comes first above a conversation. The `r` and `a` keys don't change.
- **Confirm before sending** asks "Send this message?" before each message leaves, in addition to Undo send.
- **Settings › Inbox tabs** turns Social, Promotions, Updates and Forums on or off for each account. Mail from a hidden tab shows in Primary and alerts like Primary mail.
- **Settings › Notifications › Schedule** keeps new-mail alerts to work hours, or turns them off. Outside those hours mail arrives quietly.
- **Clear downloaded mail** in Settings › Appearance › Privacy removes downloaded email bodies and attachments from this computer. Your conversation list and labels stay, and emails download again when you open them.
- **Settings › About › Help › Open guide**, or "Open setup guide" in the command palette (`mod+K`), opens the Google Cloud setup guide again.

**Compose**
- Typing in To, Cc or Bcc suggests people from your mail, with those you write to first. Arrows move through them, `Enter` or `Tab` picks one, and `Esc` hides the list.
- Suggestions can include your Google Contacts too. **Settings › Contacts** walks each account through turning on the People API and signing in again. Without it, suggestions still come from your mail.

**Sign-in codes**
- Emails with a verification or login code show it on a card above the conversation, with a **Copy code** button. `shift+C` copies it too.
- In the list, codes under an hour old show in a small chip. Click it to copy the code without opening the email.

**Reading mail**
- New-mail alerts arrive within about 10 seconds instead of up to 90.
- `Cmd+C` copies text you highlight in an email on the Mac again.

**Heads-up**
- This version updates the local mail cache (`009_contacts.sql`). Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
