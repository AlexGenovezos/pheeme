<!-- Release notes for v0.22. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Mail stats in Settings, replies from the alias the mail came to, Send later through Gmail's own Schedule send, and one-click Join for Zoom, Teams and Webex.

**Mail stats**
- A new page, **Settings › Mail stats**, shows how many emails you get a day and how many you opened, replied to or starred, over 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or a year, for all accounts or one.
- A bar per day (per week for a year) splits the mail you engaged with from the mail you never opened. Hover a bar, or use the arrow keys, to read it.
- **Where it lands** shows each inbox tab and your top labels, and **Top senders** lists who sends you the most and how much of it you read, with an **Unsubscribe** button where the sender offers one.
- It's all counted from the mail already on your computer, so the page doesn't use any of your Gmail limit. "Opened" counts mail you read anywhere, including on your phone.

**Compose**
- Mail that came to one of your Gmail "Send mail as" addresses is now answered from that address. When you have more than one address, the From row is a picker, and with Gmail's signature chosen, each address brings its own.
- **Send later in Gmail…** in the menu beside Send saves the message as a Gmail draft and opens Gmail's Drafts in your browser on the right account. Open the draft there and choose Schedule send. The schedule lives in Gmail, so every device sees it.

**Calendar**
- Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, FaceTime and other video calls get the same one-click **Join** as Google Meet: in the event popover (`shift+j`), on email invitations, and as a Join button on reminder notifications. Events with a video call show a small camera before their title.
- Quick add reads "lunch with mike murray" as Mike Murray when that name is in your contacts, so you don't have to capitalise it.
- Every two-finger swipe now steps the calendar, however fast you swipe, and the new days slide in without a jump.
- Create is now the same size and in the same place as New message, and the Mail | Calendar switch stays centred in the sidebar, including in full screen.

**Mail**
- **Unread only** now stays on when you switch mailboxes or Inbox tabs, and after a restart, until you turn it off.
