<!-- Release notes for v0.9. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Search your mail instantly, set up another device without copying credentials by hand, and keep diagnostic logs automatically.

**Search**
- Press `/` to search. Cached matches appear as you type, while Gmail finds matching mail that isn't on your machine yet.
- Search all accounts or choose one. Gmail results join the list without moving the matches already on screen, and you can load more results.
- Use Gmail filters such as `from:`, `subject:`, `has:attachment` and `is:unread`. Queries the local cache can't answer are sent to Gmail.
- Your last 20 searches are saved. Pick one to search again, or forget it from the recent-search list.
- Press `Enter` to move into the results. Clear the search or press `Escape` to return to your previous mailbox. Reading and mail actions work in search results too.

**Set up another device**
- In **Settings › Accounts › Other devices**, send your Google Cloud client ID and secret directly to another Pheeme installation on your local network.
- On the new device, choose **Receive from another device** during setup and enter the transfer code. Approve the connection on the sending device before credentials are sent.
- Transfers are encrypted, expire after five minutes and accept one receiver. You still sign in to Google on the new device; account sessions and cached mail aren't transferred.

**Diagnostics**
- Logging is on by default, including when Pheeme opens from Finder, the Start menu or a desktop launcher. Sync errors and Gmail rate-limit reasons, retries and delays stay available after you close the app.
- Logs stay on your computer. Files rotate after 5 MiB, keeping the current file and six archives, and pending entries are flushed on exit.
- On macOS, find them in `~/Library/Logs/app.pheeme.desktop/`. Request authorization headers, OAuth secrets and message bodies aren't added to the logs.

**Reading and settings**
- Images load automatically when you open a message, including quoted originals. Collapsed messages wait until you expand them.
- Attachment downloads keep moving during background sync, with **Downloading…** shown until saving finishes.
- The Trash badge counts all conversations, including mail you've already read.
- If Gmail rejects a mail action, its full explanation stays visible until you dismiss it. You can select the text to copy it, and the reason is saved in the diagnostic log.
- The list header and sidebar now use the same unread totals, so loading another page doesn't change the header's count.
- Conversations fill the available reading pane, and the empty reader's shortcut hints stay together.
- **Settings › About › What's new** shows this and previous releases' notes, even offline, with your running version marked.
- The app and website favicons now use the statue engraving to match the desktop icon.

**Heads-up**
- This version updates the local mail cache (`004_search.sql`). Once it has run, older versions can't open the cache, so keep auto-update on.
