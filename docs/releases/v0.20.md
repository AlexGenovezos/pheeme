<!-- Release notes for v0.20. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Type an event as a sentence, find text in an email with ⌘F, a cleaner calendar, and less battery on the Mac.

**Calendar**
- Press `c` (or **Create**) and type the event as a sentence, like "Meet with Kevin tomorrow at 9 am" or "Lunch every other Tue at Joe's /work". The words read as the time, repeat, length, place, people and calendar are highlighted as you type, with a preview of the event.
- Names after "with" offer matching contacts, invited only if you pick one; a typed email address is invited straight away. Start with `todo` or `remind me to` to add a Google Task instead. The toast has Undo, and **More options** opens the full editor filled in.
- Switching between Day, Week and Month no longer makes the events flash in.
- The Mail | Calendar switch sits clear of the macOS window buttons, the mini calendar fits its days, narrow events show their title, and declined events are easier to read.

**Mail**
- ⌘F (Ctrl+F on Windows and Linux) finds text in an open email: every match is highlighted, Enter steps through them, and the bar shows "3 of 12".
- Zoom and Teams meeting passcodes no longer show up as sign-in codes.

**Everywhere**
- Pheeme's own date, time and dropdown pickers replace the system ones in the event editor, Tasks, Settings and search.
- In a small window the sidebar hides instead of squashing, and mail shows one pane at a time when the list and the reading pane don't both fit.
- Buttons, checkboxes and ticks are bigger and easier to hit.

**Speed and battery**
- In the background Pheeme wakes the Mac about 5× less often: on a MacBook Air, CPU dropped from 1.2% to 0.5% and Energy Impact from 1.4 to 0.5.
- Moving through the list with `j` / `k` does about half the work.

**Fixes**
- While a Mac sleeps with Power Nap on, sync waits quietly for the keychain instead of failing every 90 seconds, and queued changes wait instead of failing.
