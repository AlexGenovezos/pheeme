<!-- Release notes for v0.14. The release workflow publishes this file as the GitHub release text. -->

Fixes from the label and notification review: Trash works again, label edits are instant, and your clicks no longer wait behind sync.

**Labels**
- Pick a colour when you create a label, from the same Gmail palette as **Label colour…**.
- Hiding a label in the label list now takes its sublabels under **More** with it.
- The `l` (**Label as…**) and `v` (**Move to…**) pickers show sublabels indented under their parent. Searching matches the full path and shows the parent beside each match.
- Hiding, recolouring and renaming a label show at once. If Gmail refuses, only that change is undone and Pheeme tells you why.

**Speed**
- Your actions (labels, archive, trash, send, search) now go ahead of the background mailbox download. When Gmail says the account is busy, the download pauses for a minute so your clicks get through.
- Holding an arrow key scrolls the open email smoothly instead of in jumps.

**Reading**
- Reply, Reply all and Forward sit in the reading pane's toolbar, so they stay in reach on long emails.
- Tags in All Mail and search use the colour of the matching sidebar icon.

**Fixes**
- Moving a conversation to Trash works again (Gmail had started refusing the request).
- Gmail errors show a short reason instead of a page of HTML, and the close button sits in the toast's top-right corner.
- Mail you send to yourself now alerts when it lands unread in your Inbox. Your own replies still don't.

**Settings**
- **Keyboard shortcuts** opens with a keyboard map: used keys are coloured by group, free keys fade out, and clicking a key shows its command so you can rebind it.
- **Diagnostics** has its own page, and **What's new** shows each release as its own card.
