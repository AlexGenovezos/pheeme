Most email clients wait on a server. pheeme keeps your mail on your machine, so opening, searching, and archiving happen as fast as you can type. When something's slow, that's a bug.

Hands stay on the keys.

Every action has a shortcut, and the ones you already know from Vim and Gmail work on day one.

j kMove through messages
eMark done and move on
r aReply, reply all
/Search everything
⌘ KRun any command

Yours to read and fork.

Your inbox is the most private thing on your computer. You should be able to read every line of the app that opens it. pheeme is open source, so you can.

Why it's called pheeme

In Greek myth, Pheme was the voice that carried the news.

She had wings and a trumpet, and word traveled as fast as she could fly.

We borrowed her name, added an e, and pointed her at your inbox.

Built in public

Here's the plan, in order. Each one gets stamped when it ships.

  1. Name, logo, and this site

    You're looking at it.

  2. Gmail sync

    Your Gmail accounts synced to a local cache, so mail opens instantly and works offline.

  3. The keyboard inbox

    Try it above: categories, threads, and actions that land before Gmail answers.

  4. Compose

    Write, reply and forward, with drafts saved to Gmail and undo send.

  5. Search

    Instant search over the mail on your machine, with Gmail's results merged in.

  6. Labels and notifications

    Manage labels, and hear about new mail from the tray.

  7. Version 1.0

    Signed builds and the polish to make pheeme your only mail client.

  8. Calendar

    Google Calendar and Tasks, next to your mail.
