Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version
  • pheeme is an email client that runs on your computer. Your mail is downloaded to your device and stays there.
  • We don't run servers that receive, store, or read your email.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your email to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect your account and delete everything pheeme stored at any time.

Who we are

pheeme is an open-source email client developed by Alex Genovezos ("we", "us"). This policy covers the pheeme desktop app (the "app") and the website at pheeme.app (the "site"). The app's source code is public at github.com/AlexGenovezos/pheeme, so you can check what it does with your data.

Data the app accesses

When you connect an email account, such as a Google account, the app asks for permission to access that account. Depending on the permissions you grant, the app can access:

  • Your email address and basic profile (name and profile picture), to identify the account in the app.
  • Your email messages, attachments, drafts, labels, and their metadata (such as senders, recipients, subjects, and dates), so you can read, search, and organize your mail.
  • The ability to send mail and change messages (for example archiving, starring, labeling, or deleting), so the actions you take in the app happen in your account.

How the app uses that data

The app uses your data only to provide the email features you see in the app: showing your mailbox, searching it, sending and replying to mail, and keeping your changes in sync with your email provider. It does not use your data for anything else.

The app talks directly from your device to your email provider (for Google accounts, Google's servers). Your messages are not routed through, copied to, or stored on any server we operate.

Where your data is stored

The app keeps a copy of your mail and a search index on your device so it can work quickly and offline. The sign-in tokens that let the app access your account are also stored on your device. We never receive them.

Your email provider keeps its own copy of your mail under its own privacy policy. For Google accounts, that is the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent, or share your data, and we don't transfer it to anyone. Because your mail never reaches us, we have nothing to share. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't allow anyone else to.

No person at pheeme reads your email. The only exception would be content you choose to send us yourself, for example if you paste part of a message into a bug report.

Google user data

pheeme's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing email features of the app.
  • We don't transfer Google user data to third parties, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice.
  • We don't use Google user data to serve ads, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based ads.
  • We don't allow humans to read Google user data, unless you give us explicit permission for specific messages, it is needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or it is required by law.
  • We don't use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models.
  • We don't sell Google user data, and we don't use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes.

Analytics and telemetry

The app does not collect analytics or usage telemetry. If we ever add crash reporting, it will be off unless you turn it on, it will never include the contents of your email, and we will update this policy first.

The website

pheeme.app is a static site. It doesn't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts, and it has no sign-up forms. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Links to GitHub take you to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Remove the account in the app. This deletes the mail, search index, and sign-in tokens pheeme stored on your device for that account.
  • Revoke access. For Google accounts, open your Google Account's third-party access page and remove pheeme. The app can no longer reach your account after that.
  • Uninstall the app. This removes the app and its local data from your device.

Deleting data in pheeme doesn't delete anything from your email provider. Your mail stays in your account.

Security

The app connects to email providers over encrypted connections and stores sign-in tokens in your operating system's secure credential storage where available. Your local mail is as safe as your device, so we recommend using disk encryption and a screen lock.

Children

pheeme is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change affects how the app handles your email, we'll also note it in the app's release notes.
